Who We Are

About ARO

The African Reclaimers Organisation is a mass-based, democratic organisation built and led by reclaimers themselves.

About ARO

ARO is a mass-based democratic organisation of reclaimers, established in 2018. We call ourselves "reclaimers" — not "waste pickers" — because we are reclaiming both the recyclable materials that would otherwise go to landfill, and our dignity as workers doing essential work for South Africa. Our vision is a waste economy that recognises, includes, and fairly pays the reclaimers who already do most of the work. ARO Recycling operates alongside the organisation to put this vision into practice.

History

Reclaimers began organising in 2016 in response to threats against their livelihoods, culminating in the first-ever march of Johannesburg's landfill and street reclaimers. That founding action grew into the mass-based movement that is ARO today.

Structures

ARO is organised through 42 local branches across Johannesburg, each electing 2 representatives to sit on ARO's coordinating committee, which in turn elects an executive committee. ARO also has members organising in Mpumalanga and the Cape Town area.

The Movement's Heart

About Reclaimers

It is estimated that between 60,000 and 90,000 people work as informal reclaimers in South Africa. They collect the majority of used recyclables that are recycled in this country.

Who Are Reclaimers

Reclaimers are the people who collect, sort, and sell recyclable materials from streets, depots, and landfills — work that keeps South Africa's cities clean and its recycling industry running, largely without recognition or fair pay.

Reclaimers' Contributions

Reclaimers collect an estimated 80–90% of used recyclables that are recycled in South Africa (2015 estimate), saving municipalities hundreds of millions of rands in landfill costs every year while diverting waste that would otherwise pollute the environment.

Stand With Reclaimers

Support

There are several ways to support ARO and the reclaimers who keep South Africa clean.

Recycling with Reclaimers

Put out your recyclables in a way that reclaimers can access, or contact ARO to join the Recycling with Reclaimers programme in your area.

Solidarity Support

Businesses and organisations can partner with ARO, buy from reclaimers, and ensure recycling contracts support the people already doing the work.

Donate

Financial support helps ARO organise, campaign, and run programmes for reclaimers nationally. Contact us to find out how to donate.
